# DAASTAAN — Design Case Study

## 1. Project Overview

DAASTAAN is an immersive digital storytelling experience celebrating Punjab's culture, heritage and identity.

## 2. Problem Statement

Cultural stories, traditions and historical experiences deserve engaging digital spaces where people can explore and connect with them.

## 3. Proposed Solution

DAASTAAN presents an interactive digital experience through storytelling, cultural exploration and intuitive navigation.

## 4. Core Features

- TimeLens — Explore stories through time.
- Voices of Punjab — Discover cultural voices and experiences.
- Interactive Navigation — Explore the platform easily.
- Cultural Visual Gallery — Discover Punjab's heritage and artistic identity.

## 5. Design Approach

The interface focuses on visual storytelling, usability, immersive presentation and intuitive navigation.
