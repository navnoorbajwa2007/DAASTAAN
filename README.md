# DAASTAAN — Stories of Punjab

An immersive digital experience celebrating Punjab's culture, heritage, voices and stories through interactive design.

## About the Project

DAASTAAN is a digital storytelling experience designed to bring Punjab's cultural identity, history and human stories closer to people through an engaging and interactive interface.

## Key Features

- *TimeLens:* Explore stories and moments through time.
- *Voices of Punjab:* Discover the voices, experiences and stories of Punjab.
- *Interactive Navigation:* Navigate the experience through an intuitive interface.
- *Immersive Design:* A visually engaging digital experience.
